The Brazilian Climate

The climate of Brazil comprises a wide range of weather conditions across a large area and varied topography, but most of the country is tropical. According to the Köppen system, Brazil hosts six major climatic subtypes: desert, equatorial, tropical, semiarid, oceanic and subtropical. The different climatic conditions produce environments ranging from equatorial rainforests in the north and semiarid deserts in the northeast, to temperate coniferous forests in the south and tropical savannas in central Brazil.

Equatorial

Amazon Basin

Found in the Amazon region, characterized by high humidity and heavy rainfall year-round. There is no real dry season, and temperatures average above 25°C consistently.

Avg Temp 25°C - 27°C
Rainfall > 2000 mm/yr
Humidity > 80%

Tropical

Central Brazil

Dominates the central part of the country. Defined by distinct wet and dry seasons. Temperatures are high, with a dry winter season from May to September.

Avg Temp 20°C - 28°C
Rainfall 1500 mm/yr
Humidity 60% - 80%

Semiarid

Northeast

Specific to the Northeast interior (Sertão). Rainfall is scarce and irregular, often leading to droughts. It is the hottest and driest region in the country.

Avg Temp 27°C +
Rainfall < 800 mm/yr
Humidity Low

Highland Tropical

Southeast Plateaus

Found in higher altitudes of the Southeast. Temperatures are milder due to elevation. Frost can occur in winter.

Avg Temp 18°C - 25°C
Rainfall 1500 mm/yr
Elevation > 800m

Subtropical

Southern Brazil

Located south of the Tropic of Capricorn. This region has four distinct seasons, with cold winters (can see frost/snow) and hot summers.

Avg Temp < 20°C (Winter)
Rainfall Distributed
Seasons 4 Distinct

Tropical Atlantic

Coastline

Coastal strip from the Northeast to the South. Humid and warm with rainfall distributed throughout the year.

Avg Temp 25°C
Rainfall 1200 - 2000 mm
Humidity High

Seasonal Dynamics

Summer (Dec - Mar)

Hot across the country, with heavy rains in the Center-West, Southeast, and South. The Northeast coast is sunnier.

Winter (Jun - Sep)

Mild to cold in the South and Southeast. The Center-West enters its dry season. The Northeast coast receives its main rainfall.
